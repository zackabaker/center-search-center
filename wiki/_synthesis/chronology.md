# Intellectual Chronology

Reading the dated archive sequentially reveals something a chronological index cannot: not just *what* was thought, but *how* the thinking moved — which problems generated new vocabulary, which concepts refused resolution and kept returning, and where the work pivoted rather than merely accumulated.

---

## Early 2021 — Stacking and Idiom-Building

The opening months are oriented toward **synthesis across existing frameworks**. [[Stacking Against the Big Scene]] establishes the [[grammatical stack]] as a structural metaphor, while [[Writing as Technics]] reaches toward deep historical grounding in ancient inscription. The goal stated explicitly in [[Toward a Unified Idiom]] is to bring the discourse of [[Anthropomorphics]] and [[technics]] into a single coherent language. The thinking at this stage is primarily *integrative* — assembling resources before pressing forward.

---

## Mid-2021 — Hypothesis, Mimesis, and the Political

From June through August, attention turns outward: what does the [[originary hypothesis]] *do* in the world? [[The Prospects of the Hypothesis]] and [[The Transdisciplinarity of the Hypothesis]] argue that the hypothesis cannot be housed inside existing disciplines and must be evaluated on its own terms. [[In the Image: Towards an Emulation Economy]] pushes [[mimesis]] from anthropological concept toward social-economic model, and [[GAE, Unironically]] tests the hypothesis against contemporary political analysis. This is a period of *application under pressure* — the concepts are being stress-tested against real-world cases.

---

## Late Summer–Fall 2021 — Intelligence, Event, and Scene

A sustained cluster of posts — [[Petitioning Technology]], [[Translating Infrastructure]], [[Being Like Data; the Central Intelligence]], [[Event Intelligence]], [[Scenic/Event Intelligence]] — develops what might be called the **intelligence-technics nexus**. [[Bernard Stiegler]]'s externalization of memory is engaged and reframed; the [[scene/event]] duality emerges as a new analytical axis. By [[Revivalistics]] and [[Originary Hypothesis as Mobius Strip]], the hypothesis itself becomes an object of meta-reflection: it is self-subverting, initiatory, and resistant to simple transmission. The thinking grows more recursive.

---

## Late 2021 — Scale, Practice, and the Self

[[Ergodism]], [[Scale]], [[Figuring Things Out]], and [[Selving]] mark a shift toward *scale and agency*. [[Scale]] explicitly names a dissidence from Eric Gans's mainstream GA, distinguishing different levels of analysis. [[Selving]] introduces [[practice]] in the MacIntyrean sense, grounding the originary scene in something like lived formation. [[Scenic/Linguistic Undecidability]] confronts Gans's "semantic leniency" directly. The cluster suggests growing confidence in departing from the received framework when necessary.

---

## Early 2022 — Inscription Becomes Central

From January through May 2022, [[inscription]] consolidates as the master concept. [[Inscription]], [[Infiltrative Inscription]], and [[Inscripto-punctualism]] develop progressively finer accounts of how marks, data, and scenes relate. [[David Olson]]'s originary prose scene recurs as a touchstone. Alongside this, [[Resentment]], [[Negative Capability]], and [[Originary Reflections on Eden]] work through affective and theological dimensions — the hypothesis is tested against scripture, against Keats, against Peircean epistemology ([[Language, the Ultimate Source and Analysis of Data]]). [[Originary Calculus]] and [[Originary Style]] suggest that the project is becoming something like a *method*, not just a theory.

---

## Spring 2022 — Scenes, Disciplines, Infrastructure

[[Media as Infrastructural Translation]], [[Disciplinarity and the Center]], and [[Converting Assets to Data: Tributarianism]] extend the scene-analysis into institutions and economies. The **ritual → juridical → scientific** scene sequence becomes a periodizing framework in its own right. The work is moving toward a general account of how [[the center]] operates across social scales.

---

## Undated Texts — Where They Likely Fit

The 498 undated texts (GABlog, book manuscripts, PDFs) likely span the full period but cluster in two zones: **foundational exposition** (material explaining the originary hypothesis in more stable, pedagogical terms — probably pre-2021 or running parallel to early Substack work) and **extended elaborations** of concepts like [[the scenic imagination]], [[grammaticality]], and [[resentment]] that appear in the dated posts as already-developed. Book-length manuscripts likely represent attempted *consolidations* that fall between the more exploratory Substack periods — stitching together what the posts kept reopening.

---

## What Changed?

The arc begins with an integrative ambition — assembling [[technics]], [[media]], and [[the originary hypothesis]] into a unified idiom — and ends somewhere more generative and unsettled: a method of [[inscription]]-analysis capable of reading institutions, economies, and selves through the logic of the [[scene]]. Along the way, the relationship to Gans's GA shifted from extension to productive dissent, the concept of [[intelligence]] was substantially reworked through [[Stiegler]] and [[Peirce]], and the political dimension moved from peripheral application to structural concern. What drives the thinking throughout is less argument than *pressure on terms* — each post tends to arrive somewhere its opening question did not anticipate, which may itself be the most originary feature of the method.
