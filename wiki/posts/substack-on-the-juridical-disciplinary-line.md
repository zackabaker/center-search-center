## Summary

This essay traces the genealogy of the [[juridical]] as a mode of deferral that emerged to contain the [[vendetta]] and residual resentment, yet remains forever marked by the vengeance it displaces. The author argues that progressive and utopian projects alike have sought to eliminate the juridical in favor of purely [[disciplinary]] rationality—replacing punishment with prevention, rehabilitation, and social engineering. However, modern data collection and surveillance technologies force a reconsideration of the juridical's protective function: the [[juridical]] sacralizes the individual against totalizing state convergence, a protection now destabilized when information is gathered routinely rather than through warrant-based search.

## Key Concepts

- **[[Juridical]]** – A mode of deferral addressing resentment through formalized punishment scaled to victim satisfaction rather than rational prevention
- **[[Disciplinary]]** – Rationalized systems of prevention, rehabilitation, and population management divorced from vengeance logic
- **[[Ritual]]** – The originary institutional form, foundational to both juridical and disciplinary modes
- **[[Vendetta]]** – The primal practice of blood revenge that the juridical emerged to contain and defer
- **[[Resentment]]** – The emotional substrate driving demands for punishment; what the juridical must address to maintain legitimacy
- **[[Sovereignty]]** – The state power constrained (or not) by juridical protections against totalizing surveillance
- **[[Error]]** – The juridical's vulnerability to miscarriage of justice; the blind spot progressives exploit to rationalize its replacement

## Notable Passages

"The juridical is a mode of deferral sidelining the vendetta...it remains marked by these practices which look barbaric from the standpoint of the juridical itself. It's impossible to imagine the juridical without some residue of vengeance."

"The juridical sacralizes the individual in relation to the state: the individual is protected against a rapid and totalizing convergence upon his person which would destroy any attempt to simply provide his own account and have it checked."

"What if the state already has this information, gathered by its own agencies or telecommunications companies in the routine course of everyday service provision?"

## Questions Raised

1. Can the [[juridical]] survive the elimination of its vengeance residue, or does stripping it of resentment-satisfaction render it merely a thin mask for disciplinary rationalism?

2. How do we preserve the [[juridical's]] protective sacralizing function when the state need no longer search to possess information—when data collection precedes any suspicion?

3. Is the progressive project to replace punishment with prevention fundamentally a replacement of one form of power with another, more totalizing form?
