## Summary

This post examines the escalating demands of victimary progressivism through the lens of LGBT activism, arguing that beneath claims of equality lies a deeper drive for vengeance and institutional domination. By analyzing Ross Douthat's series of pointed questions about religious institutions' tax-exempt status and institutional recognition, the author reveals how victimary movements leverage civilization's own mechanisms to eliminate space for traditional moral positions. The comment Douthat quotes exposes the rhetorical sleight of hand: translating biological fact into grounds for total institutional capture.

## Key Concepts

- [[Victimary]] – movements that claim victim status to justify expanding power and eliminating opposition
- [[Vengeance]] – the actual driver beneath claims of equality, masked in procedural language
- [[Declarative Culture]] – power exercised through declarations of what is real or acceptable
- [[Religious Freedom]] – increasingly hollowed as a concept when tested against victimary institutional demands
- [[Tax-Exempt Status]] – the mechanism by which the state can enforce ideological conformity
- [[Resentment]] – the psychic fuel of movements that cannot rest on achieved equality
