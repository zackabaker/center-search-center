# Little Big Men

## Summary

This essay diagnoses the contemporary Western civilizational conflict as fundamentally rooted in the ancient [[Big Man]] problem—now played out between [[producerist]] and [[consumerist]] orientations. Global managerial elites aim to reduce humanity to consumption subjects, but this concentration of power into impersonal systems (democracy, markets, science) creates a totalizing center that paradoxically weakens itself by diffusing responsibility. The [[victimary]] emerges as a viral symptom of this breakdown, while genuine [[producer's desire]] requires recognizing one's own complicity and taking responsibility for one's attention and responses.

## Key Concepts

- [[Big Man]] – the ancient proto-leader whose seizure of centrality generates marginal resentment rather than eliminating desire for centrality
- [[producerist/consumerist split]] – the fundamental divide between those who create and those defined solely by consumption choices
- [[center and margin]] – the originary geometric relation that concentrates resentment when collapsed into a single totalizing center
- [[victimary]] – the cultural virus unleashed by breakdown of pre-modern immunological systems; complaint masquerading as critique
- [[declarative systems]] – impersonal institutional structures (democracy, markets, bureaucracy) that offload responsibility onto transcendent principles
- [[responsibility]] – the key to producer's desire; the capacity to assess one's own role in enabling harmful actions
- [[managerial class]] – the contemporary priesthood who deflect accountability onto external enemies and procedures

## Notable Passages

> "The symmetry of all on the margins in relation to a single center eliminates a lot of smaller resentments, against smaller and overlapping centers, only to concentrate them all into one totalizing one: the all against one (and one against all) of tyranny."

> "The victimary, the SJWs, are just a virus released by the final breakdown of all pre-modern immunological systems—it has taken a long time to extricate the natural sense of responsibility."

> "Producer's desire begins in the realization that the world is your construct...you can assess your own behavior, and the role it has perhaps played in rendering action you now see to be harmful effective."
