## Summary

This post proposes that the three foundational GA categories—[[ritual]], [[juridical]], and [[disciplinary]]—can be formalized as programmable systems by identifying their thresholds and translating them into natural semantic primes. The author argues that intelligent [[scenic design]] requires programming likenesses across events while punctuating them with same/other cuts, enabling data exchange with central intelligence rather than seizure. The ultimate goal is a cybernetic infrastructure where adjustments in one category respond to crises in others, producing orderly [[succession]] with minimal resentment.

## Key Concepts

- **[[Threshold]]**: The point where each category (ritual, juridical, disciplinary) transitions from dysfunction to legitimate operation
- **[[Scenic design]]**: Converting furnishings into scenes and scenes into furnishings to maximize pedagogical platforms
- **[[Succession]]**: Singularized succession in perpetuity; the orderly movement toward future states
- **[[Vendetta]]**: The pre-juridical state of reciprocal violence that law converts into cases and claims
- **[[Differend]]**: The gap between imperative and execution; tyranny emerges when this oscillates uncontrolled
- **[[Central intelligence]]**: The coordinating mechanism that receives data exchange rather than suffering data seizure
- **[[Likenesses]]**: Structural similarities across scenes/events that enable generalization and programming

## Notable Passages

"Programming for singularized succession in perpetuity creates data exchange with the central intelligence rather than data seizure."

"If succession proceeds in an orderly manner, judgments are rarely indefensible enough to arouse resentments, and inquiries are restricted to how imperatives from the center can be ramified through the infrastructure."

"We want as close as possible to a series of if…then sequences that would identify at different scales as many similarities as possible while being able to make the cut at any point."

## Questions Raised

1. How can natural semantic primes (before/after, part/whole, doing/happening) actually be encoded into governance systems without collapsing into either pure ritual repetition or authoritarian imperative?

2. If the three categories are cybernetically tethered, what mechanisms detect which category is approaching crisis first, and how does the system prioritize which adjustment to make?

3. Can "data exchange" with central intelligence genuinely avoid the resentment that comes from being selected as one possible "someone like you," or is this selection always felt as an erasure of the singular self?
