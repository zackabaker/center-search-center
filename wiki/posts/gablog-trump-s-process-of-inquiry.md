## Summary

This post argues that contemporary American "fascism" is better understood as a *response* to leftist division-making rather than a primary ideological force. The author distinguishes between European fascism (perpetual war-mobilization) and American "folk fascism"—the sovereign authority's systematic enforcement of order over liberal accommodation. Rather than Nazi-style ideology, this spectral fascism emerges when authorities consistently prioritize order at every decision point where liberalism would hesitate, as in crackdowns on Antifa or illegal immigration. The paradox is that such consistent enforcement would ultimately erode the liberalism it claims to protect.

## Key Concepts

- [[Spectral Fascism]] — sovereign enforcement of order over liberal hesitation; the "real kernel" of authority in even liberal societies
- [[Folk Fascism]] — popular cultural preference for vigilante justice and rogue enforcement over strict rule-following (Dirty Harry, Paul Kersey)
- [[Sovereign Authority]] — the state's capacity to decisively isolate and expel sources of political division
- [[The Left as Extortion Racket]] — leftist strategy of manufacturing controversy and moral emergency to justify intervention
- [[Order vs. Liberalism]] — the perpetual tension between administrative decisiveness and rights-based accommodation
- [[Division-Making]] — the left's tactical creation of irreconcilable conflicts around previously uncontroversial practices
