## Summary

Gans argues that the [[originary scene]] never truly closes but continues infinitely through successive scenes that reference and confirm it. Rather than regressing endlessly, humanity engages in infinite progression—each generation performs anew on the same originary scene. This framework illuminates how [[succession]] at the [[center]] becomes the mechanism for prolonging the chain of references back to origin. The exchange between [[center and periphery]]—recognition and obedience for existence and protection—structures all subsequent ritual, kinship, language, and governance, establishing the fundamental asymmetry from which modern political conflicts emerge.

## Key Concepts

- [[Originary scene]]: The founding human event of deferral and representation that, rather than closing, persists through all subsequent cultural formations
- [[Center and periphery]]: The asymmetrical spatial relationship where the center provides and the periphery recognizes, materialized in ritual exchange
- [[Succession]]: The marking, validation, and authentication of continuity that prolongs the chain back to the originary event
- [[Sparagmos]]: The tearing apart of the central victim whose deferral generates the first sign and human language
- [[Ritual]]: Materialization of center-periphery exchange; the return of sacrificial portion to the center
- [[Big Man]]: The human usurpation of the center's sacred position, revolutionizing the originary distribution and generating all modern political ideology
- [[Sacrality]]: The aura surrounding the center that modern desecration has demolished, complicating restoration of stable nomos

## Notable Passages

> "Rather than infinite regression we have infinite progression. We are still, then, on the originary scene, and the subsequent and widely distributed scene created to confirm it also index its uncertainty and the ongoing tenuousness of the human."

> "Only by ensuring, marking, validating, authenticating, etc., succession at the center can the chain of references back to the originary scene be prolonged."

> "All of our arguments trace back to this revolution, with all of us ranging ourselves on the pro or anti-Big Man side."

## Questions Raised

1. If the originary scene persists infinitely through succession-validation, how do competing claims to represent authentic succession resolve, and does this explain contemporary political deadlock?

2. Can the [[sacrality]] of the center be genuinely restored in desacralized modernity, or does any attempt necessarily become kitsch or manipulation?

3. How does this model of perpetual originary performance account for genuine breaks or discontinuities in human culture—revolutions, conversions, or civilizational collapse?
