## Summary

This essay traces how [[ritual]] functions as distribution from and returning to the [[center]], and how this mechanism transforms across social formations. Once the center loses sacral status, ritual becomes [[central intelligence]]—a system for managing what circulates and returns. The author argues that [[reciprocity]] evolves from asymmetrical tributary relations (where debt is permanent) to the symmetrical juridical order instituted by empire, which attempts to bracket the center itself beyond reciprocal obligation. Financial systems exemplify this intelligence-gathering apparatus, though they often reveal speculation rather than genuine social needs.

## Key Concepts

- **[[Center]]**: The occupant and locus of distribution; moves from meta-person to Big Man to sacral king to transcendent imperial authority
- **[[Ritual]]**: Distribution from center returning to center; the foundational mechanism for managing sameness and social coherence
- **[[Central Intelligence]]**: The checking of communication channels and tracking of returns once the center is desacralized
- **[[Reciprocity]]**: The return of distribution "in kind"; moves from asymmetrical (tributary) to symmetrical (juridical) forms
- **[[Meta-person]]**: Sahlins's term for the ritual authority in egalitarian communities whose commands require only compliance
- **[[Transcendence]]**: The Axial Age invention enabling the center to stand beyond all reciprocal obligation to subjects
- **[[Sameness]]**: That which must endure through circulation; always provisional under reciprocal arrangements
- **[[Debt]]**: Built into tributary systems; community runs permanent deficit with center by structural design

## Notable Passages

"Everything we do is an attempt to discern and follow the imperatives of the center, which we can now (while continuing to use the more accessible word 'ritual') call findings and foundings of the same."

"Debt is built in from the beginning; the community runs a permanent deficit with the center. Sameness is always provisional."

"The imperial order institutes a juridical order in order to replace this asymmetrical reciprocity with symmetry between the subjects in relation to the center, whose occupant is beyond all reciprocity."

## Questions Raised

1. How does the modern desacralized center maintain its authority to remain "beyond reciprocity" when [[transcendence]] is no longer available as a legitimating concept?

2. Can [[central intelligence]] genuinely distinguish between speculative circulation and authentic returns reflecting social needs, or is this opacity structural to financial systems?

3. What conditions would allow [[sameness]] to become more than provisional—and would this require re-sacralization or a new form of juridical order altogether?
