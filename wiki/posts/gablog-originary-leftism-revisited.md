## Summary

This post refines a definition of leftism as "obedience to the imperative to expose the products of discipline as stolen centrality." The author acknowledges that leftism grounds itself in a genuine truth: civilization's promise that increased deferral and discipline yield flourishing is empirically ambiguous at the individual level, even if macroscopically valid. However, this truth-grounding raises a paradox—why do leftists systematically lie if their core insight is sound? The post sketches a typology of leftist motivations rooted in personal experiences of civilization's failed or uncertain promises.

## Key Concepts

- [[Leftism]]: The systematic exposure of civilization's claimed benefits as fraudulent theft of natural centrality
- [[Discipline]]: The deferral capacities and civilizing restraints that civilization demands and promises to reward
- [[Centrality]]: The implicit authority, power, and recognition that civilization appears to distribute unequally
- [[Resentment]]: The almost universal emotional baseline from which leftism draws its force
- [[Civilization]]: The macro-successful but individually ambiguous system of deferred gratification and social order
- [[Imperative]]: The binding command structure that leftists experience to unmask discipline's fraudulence

## Notable Passages

> "The civilizing process makes the implicit claim that increased capacities of deferral (discipline) will increase benefits (wealth, power and enhanced and exemplary flourishing in general)."

> "Not only is it often false, insofar as individuals who have played by all the civilizing rules often fail and those who succeed often cut a lot of corners but, in principle, it can never really be proven true."

> "The leftist does not feel compelled to deny such a claim categorically—just each and every particular one."

## Questions Raised

1. If leftism is grounded in genuine ambiguities about civilization's individual rewards, what distinguishes justified skepticism about meritocracy from the systematic lying the author identifies as leftist practice?

2. Can the typology of leftist motivations (failed promises, parasitic uncertainty, etc.) be exhaustive, or are there structural leftist imperatives that exceed personal grievance?

3. How might a defender of [[civilization]] acknowledge its individual-level ambiguities without collapsing into the leftist imperative to expose all discipline as stolen centrality?
