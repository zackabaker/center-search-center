## Summary

This post explores the conditions under which [[extra-republican force]]—raw power independent of constitutional constraints—might emerge in American politics, using Trump as a concrete case study. The author argues that genuine [[sovereignty]] requires either elite consensus or the willingness of certain power-holders to break ranks with their peers. The text sketches scenarios where Trump might gather "saturating" sovereign forces by making examples of opponents, thereby reconcentrating dispersed elite power around a restored [[center]]. The fundamental question is whether any limits remain on power-holders' willingness to transgress formal democratic procedures.

## Key Concepts

- [[Extra-republican force]]: Raw power unconcerned with constitutional limits or democratic formality
- [[Sovereignty]]: Concentrated decision-making authority that can actually enforce outcomes rather than merely perform them
- [[Center and periphery]]: The relationship between elite concentrations of power and distributed peripheral forces
- [[Elite fracture]]: Division among ruling classes that creates openings for alternative power consolidation
- [[Declarative culture]]: The facades and fictions (elections, constitutions) that mask actual power distributions
- [[Constitutional limit]]: The formal boundary that some elites refuse to transgress, marking their tolerance threshold

## Notable Passages

> "There must be some portion of the divided and distributed power up top that must be respected, and cannot abide an absolute and obvious suspension of the electoral process."

> "The more Trump makes an example of one or a few, the more others will wish not to be made examples of."

> "A restoration of the center will then start to pull the peripheral into its orbit."

## Questions Raised

1. What separates the "optimistic scenario" (some elites preserve democratic form) from total power suspension? Where exactly does the line get drawn, and who determines it?

2. If [[center]] restoration requires making "examples" of opponents, at what point does this mechanism flip from elite consolidation to naked civil conflict?

3. Can [[sovereignty]] actually be restored within a [[declarative culture]] that has become too dispersed and distributed, or does saturation require abandoning the performative layer entirely?
