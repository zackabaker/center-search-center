## Summary

Resentment, foundational to Center Study analysis, must be understood economically and structurally rather than psychologically. The author argues that resentment only emerges in relation to the [[center]], and that its distribution and adjudication become increasingly complex as social orders scale from originary scenes to imperial juridical systems. All resentment is mediated by the center—there is no "pure" resentment—and the juridical order functions primarily as a mechanism for measuring and formalizing competing resentments before they ignite vendetta cycles.

## Key Concepts

- [[Resentment]]: An irreducible social force that emerges only in relation to the center; distinct from mimetic rivalry and requiring some conception of injustice
- [[The Center]]: The focal point through which all resentment is distributed and mediated; occupant exempted from juridical formalization
- [[Mimetic Rivalry]]: Pre-resentment competition within the group that drives toward crisis; not yet structured by justice or injustice
- [[Juridical Order]]: A system formalizing conflicts and measuring resentments while preserving the center's exemption from trial
- [[Distribution]]: All originary distribution from the center functions simultaneously as debt payment and loan, anticipating and deferring resentments
- [[Imperial Order]]: Complex hierarchical system generating multiple streams of resentment (within ruling groups, between center and periphery, revolutionary)
- [[Tokenization]]: The economic formalization of resentment through exchange mechanisms

## Notable Passages

> "Donate your resentment to the center"—one of the first coined idioms of center studies, can now be given a more strictly economic meaning; i.e., it can be tokenized.

> There's no pure, unmediated resentment, because resentment only comes into being in relation to the center, which means that the mimetic rivalry that drives the group toward the mimetic crisis is not yet resentment.

> The juridical is a matter of measuring resentments... the king as ultimate judge cannot be put on trial.

## Questions Raised

- If all resentment requires a center to mediate it, what happens to resentment in post-center or decentralized systems—does it simply proliferate unmeasured?
- How do we distinguish between "legitimate" and "illegitimate" resentments without resorting to either pure rationality or pure power?
- Can tokenization of resentment actually prevent vendetta cycles, or does it merely defer them by converting them into economic claims?
