## Summary

This post examines "global warmism" as a post-Auschwitz religion that might succeed where [[White Guilt]] fails—by providing genuine solutions to collective anxiety rather than merely exacerbating victimary resentment. The author argues that both ideologies represent modern attempts to acknowledge humanity's fundamental threat to itself, as revealed by the [[originary hypothesis]]. However, a crucial paradox emerges: can a community directly confront this originary insight about [[mimetic violence]] without that very awareness destabilizing the [[deferral]] mechanisms necessary for social survival?

## Key Concepts

- [[White Guilt]] - the quintessential post-Auschwitz religion that scapegoats dominant groups while acknowledging human destructiveness
- [[originary hypothesis]] - reveals humans as fundamentally self-threatening beings driven by mimetic desire
- [[religion of impending disaster]] - ideological framework channeling collective anxiety toward future catastrophe
- [[mimetic violence]] - the inherent potential for destructive imitation in human relations
- [[deferral]] - the signifying capacity that postpones or mediates violent conflict
- [[cultic aspects]] - ritual and market practices that sustain ideological belief systems
- [[victimary]] - systems organized around scapegoating and victimhood rather than solutions

## Notable Passages

> "White Guilt is extremely direct: it provides a thorough itemization of human institutions and norms that we 'know' threaten destruction because we know the violence they have already caused."

> "No particular sign of deferral can be given priority over other candidates, nothing that is simply there between us—to be modified and questioned, but still, first of all simply there."

> "If new scientific advances could weaken the warming hypothesis, by the same token such advances could never decisively refute it; after all, yet further evidence could once again secure the higher probability."

## Questions Raised

1. **The paradox of consciousness**: If widespread acceptance of the originary hypothesis reveals our mimetic danger directly, does that knowledge itself undermine the mythological fictions necessary to maintain social order?

2. **Deferral without transcendence**: In a secular age, what specific sign or practice can function as the [[center]] that mediates [[mimetic]] conflict if no universal religious or ideological framework commands genuine assent?

3. **Religion or management?**: Is global warmism viable as a *religion* (demanding faith and sacrifice) or merely a practical governance framework—and does that distinction matter for social stability?
