## Summary

Eric Gans announces the launch of the GABlog as a platform for sustaining Generative Anthropology discourse beyond the limitations of listserv communication. The blog enables more durable, accessible contributions to GA thinking. This foundational post establishes the blog's purpose: to create a permanent archive for center-focused inquiry and collaborative intellectual work within the GA community.

## Key Concepts

- [[Generative Anthropology]]: The discipline of tracing human culture to its originary linguistic gesture
- [[Center-Study]]: The investigation of how human meaning emerges from central scenes of gathering and deferral
- [[Discourse]]: The communicative space where ideas are tested, refined, and preserved
- [[Platform]]: The institutional/technological form enabling sustained exchange among thinkers
- [[Listserv]]: The preceding communication medium, understood as temporally limited and less archivable

## Notable Passages

> "By way of keeping up with the times and permitting more permanent contributions than the GA listserv: the GABlog!"

The phrasing "keeping up with the times" suggests GA thinking must adapt its media while remaining centered on originary questions—a productive tension between form and content.

## Questions Raised

1. How does the shift from ephemeral listserv exchanges to a permanent blog archive change the nature of intellectual risk-taking and revision in GA discourse?

2. What does it mean for GA—a theory rooted in the originary scene—to embrace digital platforms as the locus of its contemporary community?

3. Does the blog's permanence create new obligations around clarity and precision compared to the more spontaneous listserv mode?
