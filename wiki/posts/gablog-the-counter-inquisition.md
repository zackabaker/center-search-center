## Summary

This post reframes contemporary victimary moral panics as a form of "Inquisition"—prosecutorial discourse that manufactures crimes (racism, sexism, etc.) functioning as pure enemy-identification rather than coherent concepts. Liberalism requires a center to decenter, making it parasitic; the counter-strategy is not to mirror accusatorial tactics but to translate and expose the political imaginary driving the attacks. By laying bare the egalitarian grid's internal contradictions—showing how its own logic produces evidence against its stated aims—one occupies the discursive center and forces acknowledgment of power's fundamental imperatives.

## Key Concepts

- [[Victimary moral panics]] — prosecutorial discourse that manufactures charges ("racism," "sexism") as pure enemy-identification, functioning like Soviet "counter-revolutionary" accusations
- [[Inquisition]] — show-trial discourse presupposing guilt and using leading questions to construct pre-crimes from past offenses
- [[Liberal parasitism]] — liberalism's need to infiltrate and decenter existing institutional authority rather than build its own
- [[Counter-infiltration]] — holding the center (even as basic competence) to prevent complete institutional collapse under liberal pressure
- [[Egalitarian grid]] — the imposed framework that inflates differences while claiming to address them, but whose logic undermines its own stated goals
- [[Power gathering]] — the underlying mechanism identifying vulnerable enemies and institutions to control through discourse campaigns
- [[Discourse translation]] — patient restating of what liberal discourse actually demands, surfacing hidden political imaginary and resentment

## Notable Passages

> "Liberalism is intrinsically parasitic: it needs a center to be de-centered."

> "Accusations with follow-up questions presupposing the legitimacy of the accusation...terms like 'racism,' 'sexism,' 'homophobia,' function exactly as much like 'counter-revolutionary' did in the USSR."

> "If the grid is removed, they are providing evidence for a case very different than the one they thought they were making."

## Questions Raised

1. If mirroring the inquisitorial method fails, and discourse translation alone cannot stop institutional infiltration, what positive institutional forms could actually resist liberal parasitism without merely replicating its tactics?

2. When the egalitarian grid's contradictions are exposed (e.g., the unsolvable boundary between "toxic" and "non-toxic" masculinity), does awareness of this incoherence actually constrain power accumulation, or does power operate precisely *through* such unstabilized categories?
