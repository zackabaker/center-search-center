## Summary

Equality as a social goal is illusory; what we actually pursue is [[symmetry]]—the ability to place humans within controlled [[frames]] where irrelevant criteria are bracketed so we can assess a single dimension of ability. As societies become more [[discipline|disciplined]] and specialized, autonomous disciplines require [[symmetry]] to recruit and evaluate members fairly, creating a democratic component to [[sovereignty]] not from moral principle but from instrumental necessity. This framework explains both why modern societies enable mobility among the talented and why "equality" discourse masks persistent inequality.

## Key Concepts

- [[Symmetry]]: An "aspect" under which humans appear identical except for one measured quality; the actual goal disguised by equality rhetoric
- [[Frame]]: A controlled perspective that brackets assumptions and suspends prior knowledge to enable fair assessment
- [[Discipline]]: Systematized, autonomous practices (medicine, law, science) that require symmetry for recruitment and evaluation
- [[Bracketing]]: The methodological suspension of irrelevant criteria (heritage, race, gender, wealth) to isolate a single ability
- [[Sovereignty]]: The order dependent on disciplines; gains democratic interest in enabling mobility among talented
- [[Equality]]: Achieved disciplinary perspective, not presumed human attribute; a "chimerical" goal that organizes conflict
- [[Degree zero]]: Hypothetical point where all distorting accretions on ability have been identified and controlled

## Questions Raised

1. If symmetry is what we actually seek, does the persistent invocation of "equality" serve primarily as ideological cover—and what would politics look like if we spoke directly about [[symmetry]] instead?

2. How does the [[frame|framing]] required by one discipline (medicine) differ from that required by another (law or pedagogy), and do incommensurable framings create tension in a society trying to apply them simultaneously?

3. What happens to those who cannot or will not enter the frames that create [[symmetry]]—are they excluded from both disciplinary advancement *and* the informal symmetries of conviviality, doubly marginalized?
