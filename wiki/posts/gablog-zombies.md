## Summary

This brief post references an external essay on *Zombie Contentions* examining how groups engaged in mob behavior accuse others of the same charge—a recursive dynamic of [[resentment]] and [[mimetic desire]]. The post suggests a GA-inflected analysis of how [[victimary progressivism]] and contemporary declarative culture produce mutually-accusing collectives. The "zombie" metaphor implies thoughtless, herd-driven actors caught in cycles of reciprocal blame.

## Key Concepts

- [[Mimetic desire]] — The human tendency to desire what others desire, generating rivalry and resentment
- [[Resentment]] — The emotional fuel of victimary consciousness and moral accusation
- [[Victimary progressivism]] — Ideological framework centering victim status as moral authority
- [[Declarative culture]] — Culture organized around explicit claims and counter-claims rather than earned meaning
- [[Mob behavior]] — Thoughtless collective action driven by contagious emotion rather than reasoned discourse
- [[Reciprocal accusation]] — The logic where accuser and accused swap positions in cycles of blame

## Notable Passages

> "The pot calling the kettle mob"

This title captures the symmetry: both accuser and accused participate in mob logic, each claiming moral high ground while engaging in the very behavior condemned.
