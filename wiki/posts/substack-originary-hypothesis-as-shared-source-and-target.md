## Summary

This piece sketches a utopian endpoint where the [[originary hypothesis]] becomes universal common sense—a shared framework for understanding human meaning-making and conflict-resolution. The author argues that complete social adoption of "representation is the deferral of violence" would eliminate resentment-driven mimetic conflict while enhancing enjoyment of goods as gifts. The paradox is that the hypothesis must permeate consciousness so completely that it eventually dissolves into language itself, becoming unnecessary as a reminder precisely because forgetfulness would be impossible.

## Key Concepts

- [[Originary hypothesis]] — The founding claim that human meaning emerges from the deferral of violence through representation on a singular scene
- [[Deferral]] — The displacement of violence through linguistic and cultural representation; the core mechanism preventing collective catastrophe
- [[Mimetic desire]] — The human tendency to desire what others desire, generating rivalry and resentment
- [[Central imaginary]] — A sovereign mode of reasoning that imagines desired conditions already existing and works backward to discover what infrastructure would make them real
- [[Representation]] — The deferral of violence; the symbolic system that prevents species-ending conflict
- [[Commemorative consciousness]] — A shared awareness of humanity's common origin that structures all interaction and speech
- [[Name-of-God]] — Every word as invocation of the sacred center; language as prayer

## Notable Passages

> "Representation is the deferral of violence" would be at the tip of everyone's tongue just like a phrase like "God-given rights" is now for most Americans.

> If the originary hypothesis were to so absolutely inflame without consuming our every interaction, would it still exist? If it so permeated our thinking as to leave no other way of thinking, would we still be thinking it?

> Whoever could give you exactly what you want could also want to give you something else—the purpose of thinking along these lines is to deconstruct your desires into infrastructural clarifications rather than arbitrarily designed fantasy end products.

## Questions Raised

1. If the originary hypothesis must dissolve into language itself to succeed, how would we distinguish success from mere forgetting—and does that distinction matter?

2. How can infrastructural clarifications of desire avoid becoming their own form of arbitrary design, imposed by those who control the "central imaginary"?

3. Does the logic of complete deferral require eliminating the possibility of resentment entirely, or is some minimal residue of resentment (as in Christianity) structurally necessary to keep the system self-aware?
