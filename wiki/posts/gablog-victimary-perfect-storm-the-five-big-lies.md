## Summary

This post identifies five coordinated "Big Lies" propagated by the Left through institutional power—claims about gender, sexuality, race, Islam, and immigration that are sustained not through argument but through social enforcement and corporate censorship. The author argues these lies require "All Lies All the Time" to maintain coherence, and that their enforcement increasingly demands violence. Only organized resistance (exemplified by gun-rights advocates) has slowed their advance, suggesting the Left hesitates only when facing potential military defeat, not moral objection.

## Key Concepts

- [[victimary]] — the regime of victimhood politics that weaponizes claims of oppression to silence contrary facts
- [[Big Lies]] — coordinated falsehoods sustained through institutional power rather than rational argument
- [[declarative culture]] — a system where statements become "self-evidently true" through coordinated assertion rather than evidence
- [[All Lies All the Time]] — the logical requirement that once foundational lies are institutionalized, all supporting lies must follow to maintain consistency
- [[discourse destruction]] — the erosion of civil debate as prerequisite for sustaining lies
- [[compact resistance]] — organized minority resistance (like gun-rights lobbies) capable of deterring even well-resourced power structures

## Notable Passages
