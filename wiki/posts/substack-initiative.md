## Summary

This essay reframes capitalism not as liberal bottom-up enterprise but as a [[center]]-ordered contracting system where the state delegates imperatives through competing private firms. The modern capitalist order emerged when states mortgaged themselves to national banks, creating a global system of cascading debt and indebtedness rather than genuine agency. Real [[initiative]] exists only insofar as one occupies the [[center]] and controls succession; when states lose control of succession to financial institutions, new protocols must emerge to maintain [[sovereignty]] and prevent redistributive threats like jubilees.

## Key Concepts

- **[[Center]]** — The signifying and occupied locus of power from which all initiative and agency emanate
- **[[Initiative]]** — Genuine agency that only exists through occupying the center and managing succession protocols
- **[[Contracting]]** — The state's delegation of imperatives (military, health, infrastructure) to competing private firms as cost-free experimentation
- **[[Sovereignty]]** — The capacity to control succession and prevent systemic collapse through debt management
- **[[Debt]]** — The foundational bond linking populations, contractors, and states in the modern world order
- **[[Succession]]** — The scheduled rotation of occupancy in the center; its loss to banks necessitates new protocols
- **[[Indebtedness]]** — The systemic condition binding all layers (populations, contractors, state) into one order
- **[[World order]]** — Asymmetrical articulation of states competing under transnational sovereignty and banking control

## Notable Passages

> "The state, in this case, is primarily a debt enforcement agency, and the politics of the state of indebtedness involves attempts on the part of one sector or another to shift the load of debt onto another."

> "Agency, or initiative, only exists insofar as one becomes a center, both signifying and occupied, holding and held, and therefore working out the protocols of succession."

> "To lose the reins of succession, as begins as soon as the state is mortgaged to the banks, is to confront the necessity of creating new means of occupying the center."
