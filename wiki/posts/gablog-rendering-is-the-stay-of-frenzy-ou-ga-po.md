## Summary

This post proposes **Ou-GA-po**, a generative anthropology-specific adaptation of Oulipo (Workshop for Potential Literature), which devises formal rules for theoretical and literary inquiry. Rather than arbitrary linguistic constraints, GA-informed workshops would create *purposeful* rules that facilitate inquiry into the [[originary scene]] or clarify theoretical vocabulary. The core procedure examined here is **synonymization**: replacing key theoretical terms with synonyms to bring new conceptual fields into proximity, thereby generating new theoretical possibilities through language's iterative character.
