## Summary

This post argues that universalism and egalitarianism function as ideological poisons that attack the particular and hierarchical structures necessary to all real institutions. The author traces this pathology to [[metaphysics]]—understood as the tyranny of the [[declarative sentence]] over other linguistic forms, especially the [[imperative]]—which mistakes language for information transmission rather than [[conflict reduction]]. The sovereign's role should be preserving institutional integrity, yet sovereigns often capitulate to egalitarian pressure to bypass institutional mediation, enabled by self-replicating resentment-memes that frame all grievance as zero-sum struggle.

## Key Concepts

- [[Declarative sentence]] — The metaphysical ideal treating language as truth-discovery via disinterested exchange; colonizes other linguistic forms
- [[Imperative]] — The linguistic form expressing command and authority; systematically suppressed by metaphysical thinking
- [[Sovereignty]] — The mediating authority between institutions; should protect rather than override institutional structures for expedience
- [[Resentment]] — The emotional driver of universalist ideology; self-replicating viral memes that infect social consciousness
- [[Institutional integrity]] — The particularist, hierarchical order that preserves function; threatened by egalitarian sabotage
- [[Conflict reduction]] — Language's true function; corrupted when reframed as mere information control
- [[Metaphysics]] — The attempt to subordinate all linguistic forms to the declarative; inherently imperative-phobic

## Notable Passages

> "Universalisms and egalitarianisms are declarations of war upon anything 'particular' and any form of (real or perceived) inequality... declarations of war on whatever is at home in what is."

> "The sovereign, who should have no other concern than to protect and mediate between institutions and communities, would at times rather take a short-cut rather than engage in respectful consultation."

> "Metaphysics is the attempt to subordinate other elementary linguistic forms to the declarative, which means to eliminate them as independent forms... metaphysics is imperative-phobic."
