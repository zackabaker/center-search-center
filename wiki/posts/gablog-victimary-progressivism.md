## Key Concepts

- [[victimary]] – A social-political orientation organized around identity-based victimhood claims and their moral authority
- [[progressivism]] – Ideology claiming to advance society, often through redistributive or corrective mechanisms
- [[resentment]] – The affective and moral foundation underlying victimary consciousness
- [[declarative culture]] – Societies organized through utterance, symbolic claim-making, and linguistic authority rather than mimetic deferral
- [[power]] – The capacity to impose meaning and structure through declaration rather than force alone
