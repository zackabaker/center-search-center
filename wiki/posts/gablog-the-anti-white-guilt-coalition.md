## Summary

This post argues that recent Middle Eastern conflict has revealed a coalition of those awakening to the damage caused by "White Guilt"—a form of moral narcissism that rejects Western productivity and sides with anti-Western forces. Gans contends that White Guilt's complicity with anti-Israel and anti-Semitic sentiment stems from a deeper paradox: the West's commitment to [[firstness]] (the Hebrew monotheistic revolution) now invites its own destruction. The post contrasts Israel's economic productivity with the parasitic charity of terror organizations, suggesting that victimary progressivism systematically devalues productive civilization.

## Key Concepts

- [[White Guilt]]: Moral narcissism that confuses victimhood with righteousness and undermines Western civilization.
- [[Victimary mindset]]: A framework that elevates victim status above all other values, including productivity and truth.
- [[Firstness]]: The inaugural claim to universal truth; originally the Hebrew monotheistic revolution, now embodied in Western economic and cultural leadership.
- [[Declarative culture]]: Culture based on shared claims to meaning; opposed by those who deny productivity's worth.
- [[Moral narcissism]]: Self-righteous posture masking complicity with destructive forces.
- [[Productivity]]: The mark of a functioning civilization; reviled by victimary logic due to Nazi association.
