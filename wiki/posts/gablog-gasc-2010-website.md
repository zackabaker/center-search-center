## Summary

This brief notice announces the 4th annual Generative Anthropology Summer Conference (GASC), held June 24-26, 2010 in Salt Lake City at Westminster College and Brigham Young University. The post invites paper submissions and provides practical logistics: affordable on-campus lodging ($25/night) and nearby hotel options ($80-90/night). The announcement reflects the institutional establishment of GA as a serious academic field with regular scholarly gatherings and infrastructure for collaborative inquiry.
