## Summary

This post examines the emerging antisemitic resentment that accompanies the dismantling of post-WWII "non-discrimination" regimes and the restoration of [[ascriptive difference]]. The author argues that Western Jews will be accused of constitutional hypocrisy: defending ethnic homogeneity for Israel while promoting diversity and mass immigration in Western nations. He traces the [[mimetic]] structure of this resentment—in which Jews function as both model and rival—and shows how dissident-right thinkers, exemplified by Kevin MacDonald, reverse moral claims while adopting the very "critical" strategies they attribute to Jews.

## Key Concepts

- [[Victimocracy]]: A regime of power organized around victimary claims and non-discrimination ideology
- [[Ascriptive difference]]: Categories of identity (race, nationality, religion) that cannot be eliminated, only suppressed or transformed
- [[Resentment]]: A structural mimetic force that emerges when the model is envied and blamed simultaneously
- [[Non-discrimination regime]]: Post-WWII institutional apparatus attempting to suppress explicit ascriptive categorization
- [[Mimetic rivalry]]: The dynamic where the rival simultaneously imitates and opposes the model
- [[Cultural imperative]]: A quasi-biological drive toward group preservation disguised as universal principle
