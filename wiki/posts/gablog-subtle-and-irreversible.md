## Summary

This post examines how a determined executive administration with congressional support could effect fundamental systemic change through subtle, structural means rather than explicit confrontation. Rather than large-scale scapegoating operations, the strategy would involve staffing decisions, regulatory reinterpretation, and selective enforcement across agencies—making changes "irreversible" before resistance can organize. The author argues this represents a deeper threat to the existing order than visible ideological combat, and suggests genuine institutional reform would require dismantling Washington's permanent bureaucratic and media structures.

## Key Concepts

- [[Administrative State]] – The vast regulatory apparatus that enables transformative change through appointment and enforcement rather than legislation
- [[Scapegoating]] – The ritualized blame mechanism that can be suspended or targeted strategically to maintain legitimacy
- [[Declarative Culture]] – The power to define reality through official interpretation and enforcement (e.g., what counts as "fair")
- [[Sovereignty]] – The capacity to act without constraint; here, achieved through institutional capture rather than explicit authority
- [[Subtle Irreversibility]] – Structural changes made gradually enough that reversal becomes politically impossible
- [[Institutional Critique]] – The radical questioning of Washington's permanent establishments (CIA, State Department, media, parties)
- [[Transcendence]] – The legitimizing ideological frame that must be maintained even while transforming the order beneath it

## Notable Passages

"A government that scruples not to blow with the 'righteous wind' at its back can make very thoroughgoing 'change' without anyone really noticing until it is much too late."

"There is almost nothing actually existing in Washington, with the exception of Constitutionally mandated institutions, that I wouldn't include in this imperative."

"The difference...is not brains: it is familiarity with Washingtonese and media-speak—the ability to navigate institutional power."
