## Summary

This essay proposes a methodological innovation: designing discourse around [[50/50 judgments]] rather than settled certainties. The author argues that [[Thirdness]] can operate through prediction markets where bettors anticipate expert judgments on deliberately balanced propositions. More radically, he sketches a mode of thinking that pushes toward "50/50 all the way down"—abandoning the traditional dialectic between known and unknown that stabilizes conversation, and instead embracing [[differenda]] (Lyotard's term for incommensurable utterances) as a generative principle of inquiry.

## Key Concepts

- [[Thirdness]] — A mediating authority or mode that arbitrates between incommensurable positions through structured judgment
- [[50/50 judgment]] — A proposition set up to be genuinely undecidable in advance, generating competitive prediction and thought
- [[Idiom]] — A distinctive verbal or conceptual currency through which thought and exchange occur
- [[Differenda]] — Lyotard's concept of utterances that cannot be reconciled or judged by common measure
- [[Prediction market]] — A betting structure that establishes odds and generates surplus value (vig) while incentivizing accurate anticipation
- [[Dialectic of known and unknown]] — The conversational oscillation between shared assumptions and areas of disagreement

## Notable Passages

> "If the bettor can trust the judgment and integrity of the Thirdness team, then the construction of the case creates conditions under which the bettor exercises his somewhat less informed judgment…to anticipate the judgment of the Thirdness team."

> "To designate a particular judgment as 50/50 is to presuppose a lot of the world being far more certain than that."

> "I want to attempt now…is to abandon that logic and aim at 50/50 all the way down, both to test out a new way of thinking and to expand the range of Thirdness."

## Questions Raised

1. How can discourse remain coherent and progressive if one genuinely abandons the stabilizing dialectic between known and unknown—does differenda-all-the-way-down collapse into noise?

2. What would "holding things constant at 100/0" actually require in practice? Where does the certainty come from that enables the 50/50 to appear?

3. Is the prediction market structure merely an analogy for a new thinking, or does it have substantive institutional implications for how knowledge and judgment should be organized?
