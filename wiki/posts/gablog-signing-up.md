# Signing Up

## Summary

This essay traces how [[repetition]] becomes the fundamental problem and solution of human meaning-making. Starting from the [[originary hypothesis]]—where a single gesture concentrates all meaning at a sacred center—the author shows how early sign-users rapidly saturated their world with competing meanings, developing gestural-oral systems to ensure coherence. The transition to [[writing]] and other [[media]] allowed humans to control and modulate meaning by isolating specific features, moving from finite sacred gesture to infinite textual systems. Culture itself exists to defer violence by making [[repetition]] reliable enough to coordinate action across uncertain others.

## Key Concepts

- [[Sign]]: a meaningful unit that can be repeated as the same sign across different occasions and speakers
- [[Repetition]]: the fundamental human problem; meaning exists only insofar as signs can be reliably repeated
- [[Originary hypothesis]]: the event establishing one gesture as significant and one object as sacred, concentrating all meaning there
- [[Center]]: the shared focal point that enables meaning but whose stability can never be guaranteed in advance
- [[Gesture]]: bodily articulation (posture, accent, movement) that carries meaning through its accentuation and nullification of components
- [[Media]]: technologies like [[writing]] that allow isolation and control of specific meaning-features, enabling finite systems to become infinite
- [[Geste]]: Marcel Jousse's term for sound/gesture/posture articulations forming a self-referential, communal system of coherence

## Notable Passages

> "A sign has meaning insofar as it can be repeated, which is to say, repeated as the same sign...meaning can never be guaranteed in advance, just like you can never be sure whether a joke will fall flat."

> "The earliest sign users must have moved quickly from that one, single, meaning, to a world bursting with meaning...every animal, plant, change in the weather, etc., would be saying something."

> "It is the textual reduction of meaning that creates the infinite system."
