## Summary

This post traces metaphysics as a political innovation grounded in the false prioritization of the [[declarative sentence]] over more primordial linguistic forms like the [[ostensive sign]]. Gans shows that metaphysics—the belief that propositions (true/false) are language's primary form—emerges historically with the suppression of sacrifice and the creation of ruler-ruled relations in Ancient Greece. The text argues that [[originary thinking]] accomplishes what modern philosophy (Derrida, Heidegger) only attempted: a clean dismantling of metaphysics by recognizing language's true hierarchy without obscuring the political stakes of this genealogy.

## Key Concepts

- [[Declarative sentence]]: The propositional form (subject-predicate) mistakenly treated as language's foundation; carries implicit claim to access a higher reality of pre-existing concepts.
- [[Ostensive sign]]: The primordial linguistic gesture designating a desired object while signifying communal deferral of its appropriation.
- [[Metaphysics]]: The Western philosophical tradition grounded in belief that reality is fundamentally expressible as true/false propositions; masks its own political origins.
- [[Originary thinking]]: GA's corrective approach that traces language back to its actual originary scene, dismantling metaphysics without deconstruction's endless aporia.
- [[Political proceduralism]]: The regulation of unstable regimes (e.g., democracy-oligarchy cycles) through appeal to truth and reason as legitimacy.
- [[Sacrifice]]: The pre-metaphysical communal mechanism replaced by declarative truth-seeking in the emergence of ordered polities.

## Notable Passages

> "If all language does is accurately reflect the (non-linguistic) reality outside of it, then, like a good window or mirror…you don't notice it when it is working."

> "The replacement of the ostensive gesture with the declarative sentence as the primary linguistic form is the original 'Enlightenment'—rather than the identification of a victim to sacrifice, metaphysics proposes the arrival at the true meaning of words as a basis for community."

> "Originary thinking accomplishes the task…by simply recognizing that the true meaning of declarative [language emerges from] its actual originary scene."
