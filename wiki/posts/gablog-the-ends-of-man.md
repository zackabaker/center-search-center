## Summary

Humans originate in the deferral of [[mimetic violence]] through [[representation]], which constitutes our fundamental telos. This originary structure grounds all morality, institutions, and social cooperation—not as a utopian endpoint but as an eternal practice. The [[center]] emerges as the model for all future action, teaching us to imitate its peace-making gesture rather than each other's destructive mimesis. Once this signifying capacity is established, humans extend representation into increasingly complex cooperative domains, ultimately turning the capacity inward for [[self-mastery]].

## Key Concepts

- [[Mimetic violence]]: The specifically human intensification of desire where rivals mutually destroy through imitative escalation
- [[Representation]]: The gesture of aborted appropriation that simultaneously indicates violence averted and models cessation for others
- [[Center]]: The sacrificial object credited with averting crisis, becoming the fount of wisdom, knowledge, and the model for all future action
- [[Deferral]]: The eternal postponement (never elimination) of violence through signifying practices
- [[Self-mastery]]: The application of representational deferral inward, managing envy and resentment through identification of one's own potential violence
- [[Originary structure]]: The foundational scene establishing teleology and morality implicitly in human emergence

## Notable Passages

> "Teleology and morality are fully implicit in the originary structure. The deferral of violence through representation is what we are 'meant' to do."

> "Our telos is to desist from mimetically and rivalrously imitating our fellows by imitating the center."

> "The sign/gesture can be issued before anyone moves towards the object; it can be issued in the process of dismembering and consuming the meal; it can be issued once again afterward, to ensure and 'certify' that all has gone as it should."

## Questions Raised

1. If violence can only be deferred eternally, not eliminated, how do we account for historical periods of relative peace, or are we merely deferring at different temporal scales?

2. Does the internalization of the center through [[self-mastery]] require new institutions or mediating signs, or does individual ethical maturation follow naturally from recognizing the originary structure?

3. How does the extension of [[representation]] into "new modes of cooperation" avoid collapsing back into mimetic rivalry at each threshold of social complexity?
