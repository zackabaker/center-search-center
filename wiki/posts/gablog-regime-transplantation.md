## Summary

This post analyzes the structural impossibility of liberal democracy's self-correction, tracing how [[anti-discrimination law]] creates the ideological apparatus that sustains [[victimary progressivism]]. Rather than focusing on activist movements themselves, the author redirects attention to institutional support (corporations, foundations, universities) that enforce these regimes through hiring, employment, and social ostracism. The path forward requires the institutional elite to abandon the high-low coalition against the middle, clarifying minds about how power actually operates beneath liberal rhetorical cover.
