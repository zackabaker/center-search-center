# Some Political Reflections

## Summary

This post articulates a GA-inflected political framework organized around two axes: the **vertical axis** of command authority and the **horizontal axis** of information flow. The author argues that effective governance depends on whether imperatives are properly issued, transmitted, and received—and whether intelligence is gathered and communicated with maximum clarity and usability. Rather than debating institutional forms abstractly, the real test is functional: what structure best enables command and intelligence to intersect? The author proposes "singularized succession in perpetuity" as the operational principle that proves a functioning [[sovereignty]] while remaining radically transformative yet practical.

## Key Concepts

- **[[Vertical axis]]**: The chain of command; imperatives issued with presumption of obedience and fulfilled through clear transmission and reception
- **[[Horizontal axis]]**: The infrastructure of data, information, and intelligence that serves the command chain with maximum comprehensibility
- **[[Well-formed imperatives]]**: Commands that are situationally actionable, properly transmitted, and received as intentional by subordinates
- **[[Singularized succession in perpetuity]]**: The proof of operational authority—the ability to select one's successor such that it serves social continuity
- **[[Institutional memory]]**: The preserved operational structure and knowledge that survives across generations
- **[[Anthropomorphic politics]]**: Politics grounded in actual human capacities for issuing commands and processing intelligence, rather than abstract principles

## Notable Passages

> "Because what's the point of putting people in charge of things if they're not in charge of things?"

> "The most direct proof of a operational command structure is being able to select one's successor, and the most direct proof of an operational intelligence structure is that such selection is most likely to best serve social continuity."

> "It's also the most visionary as well as most practical politics, since it would inflame the imagination of anyone thinking of the kinds of people and relationships needed to create such an order while informing what anyone can do right now."

## Questions Raised

1. How does one maintain the clarity of the [[horizontal axis]] (intelligence/information flow) under conditions of deliberate disinformation deployed as "part of a broader intelligence operation"? When does opacity serve operational security versus when does it corrode the system?

2. Does "singularized succession in perpetuity" remain viable in mass democratic contexts where the selecting agent is distributed across millions, or does this framework necessarily imply more concentrated authority structures?

3. What mechanisms can audit institutional health without themselves becoming part of the "10% corrosive effect" that undermines the entire order?
