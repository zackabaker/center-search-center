## Summary

This brief post announces the migration or publication of new content on a separate platform called "Zombies Contentions." While minimal in itself, it reflects the distributed, multi-platform nature of contemporary intellectual discourse and the problem of audience attention fragmentation. The post raises questions about how GA thinking circulates across different media spaces and maintains coherence as a generative anthropological project.

## Key Concepts

- [[Declarative culture]]: The explicit declaration and circulation of ideas through distributed platforms
- [[Attentional economy]]: Competition for reader focus across multiple online venues
- [[Media]]: The material and institutional conditions through which thought is disseminated
- [[Community]]: The audience assumed by acts of intellectual migration or cross-posting

## Notable Passages

The entire text reads: "I've got a new post over at zombies contentions, if anyone's interested." This functions as both announcement and invitation—minimal in form but maximal in its assumption that intellectual continuity can span platforms.

## Questions Raised

1. How does the distribution of GA discourse across multiple platforms (original blog, linked sites, social media) affect the coherence and reception of generative anthropological thought?

2. What does the casual tone of "if anyone's interested" suggest about the speaker's relationship to audience authority and the stakes of intellectual attention in digital spaces?

3. Is there a GA-significant difference between centralizing thought on a single blog versus distributing it across multiple "contentions," and what does this imply for the formation of a thinking community?
