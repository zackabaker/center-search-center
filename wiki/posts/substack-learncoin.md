# Learncoin

## Summary

This text proposes reconceiving pedagogical practice as a form of currency that abolishes money from within. Rather than treating money as bets on future earnings backed by sovereign power, the author argues that learning itself—marked by idioms and transferable scenes of instruction—constitutes units of exchange that reproduce centered ordinality. The key move is treating curated samples of action and speech (data suitable for juridical proceedings) as pedagogical increments with inherent value, transforming how we understand both [[capitalism]] and the [[social center]].

## Key Concepts

- [[Money]] — A partition of the center's imperative that discounts against expected future earnings; assumes continuity of power to introduce controlled discontinuities.
- [[Center]] — Both generative origin (continued through social center) and anything sustaining joint attention; the locus toward which imperial systems redirect attention.
- [[Pedagogy]] — The process of filling the imperative gap; learning marked by removal of traces of ignorance and transmission of idioms.
- [[Idiom]] — A commemorative mark of a learning scene; transferable across contexts, infiltrating and converting other scenes.
- [[Imperium in Imperio]] — Imperial systems as centripetal forces drawing attention away from the social center toward curated samples and data.
- [[Singularized Succession]] — Vertical and horizontal transmission of power; the default mode against which discontinuities must be monitored and initiated.
- [[Declarative Sample]] — Action or speech curated as data suitable for juridical exchange; a unit of pedagogical value.

## Notable Passages

> "Money assumes the continuity of the world and then relies on that assumption to introduce discontinuities that the holder of money will be likelier than others to receive a return on."

> "Learning always involves rising above some threshold at which single practices come together in a new whole... once you realized that, all the 'pieces' would come together."

> "You make your actions clarify the imperative of the center by obeying the best version of the commands issued within your team; you make your speech free of slander, fraud and incitement so it would be of value in any proceeding."

## Questions Raised

1. How does the distinction between removing traces of ignorance (shame-driven forgetting) and consciously assembling those traces for others map onto the difference between implicit and explicit knowledge transmission in [[declarative culture]]?

2. If pedagogical increments replace money, what prevents the same hoarding and inequality dynamics from re-emerging around access to learning scenes and idioms?

3. Does treating juridically-suitable speech and action as currency units risk reducing all social practice to evidentiary performance—and is that reduction itself the price of abolishing money?
