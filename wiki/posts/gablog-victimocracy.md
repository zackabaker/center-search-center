## Summary

This post introduces "victimocracy" as a novel form of political rule in which rights and legitimacy are determined by one's status as an approved victim and one's alignment with designated oppressor groups. Rather than traditional ideological categories, victimocracy operates through a mainstreamed "race, class, and gender" framework that discredits certain speakers (particularly white men) *a priori* while exempting favored progressives from critique. The author argues this system can function as a coherent governance strategy but contains inherent instability, suggesting "exodus" as the only plausible response.

## Key Concepts

- [[Victimary logic]]: Political legitimacy grounded in claims to victimization rather than principle or interest
- [[Declarative culture]]: Culture imposed through top-down designation of approved narratives and victim categories
- [[Resentment]]: The affective engine driving victimocratic governance and its coalition
- [[Sovereignty]]: The question of who guards the guardians in a victimocratic regime
- [[White maleness]]: A code category encompassing normalcy itself, now subject to systematic delegitimation
- [[Exodus]]: The author's proposed response to an unsustainable political order

## Notable Passages

> "Rights, under this regime, are defined by one's claim to victimization, or by having oneself deemed an honorary victim, and legitimate arguments are those which defend some approved victim against an officially designated oppressor group."
