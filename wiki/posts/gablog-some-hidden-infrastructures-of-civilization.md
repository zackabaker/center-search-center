# Some Hidden Infrastructures of Civilization

## Summary

The author argues that liberal democratic discourse obscures the primitive tribal and power relations that underlie all civilization. Contemporary politics has shifted from class-based conflict to identity-based conflict, where victimary logic—particularly anti-white sentiment among "goodwhites"—drives political division. When government weaponizes anti-discrimination ideology against portions of its own population, it inevitably generates tribal backlash, as seen in Trump's rise. Civilization functions as a "veneer" that restrains barbaric potentialities, but this veneer becomes unstable when turned reflexively against itself.

## Key Concepts

- [[Victimary]] – Political logic centered on identifying and protecting designated victim groups, now indexed to racial and ethnic identity rather than class
- [[Civilization]] – A fragile veneer of norms and disciplines masking more primitive tribal and power relations beneath
- [[Barbarism]] – The tribal, ethnically-conscious identity politics emerging when civilization's constraints are loosened
- [[Declarative culture]] – Government-imposed categories and enforcements (anti-discrimination) that structure social reality
- [[Goodwhites]] – Eric's term for whites who have internalized anti-white victimary consciousness
- [[Nationalism]] – The reassertion of ethnic and cultural particularity against globalist homogenization
- [[Ethnomasochism]] – Self-hatred indexed to one's own ethnic or racial identity

## Notable Passages

> "Civilization is never anything more than veneer. This assertion doesn't downplay the importance of civilization; quite to the contrary: the veneer keeps all kinds of human potentialities at bay."

> "Once the government decides its job is to fight discrimination, it goes to war against part of the population it supposedly represents, and with ever more precision and comprehensiveness."

> "You cannot help but induce that part of the population to find itself at war with the government, and also against the beneficiaries of government patronage."
