## Summary

This essay explores the paradox that the perfection of any ordering system—the [[juridical]], [[ritual]], or [[disciplinary]]—tends toward its own abolition. By centering the juridical as a mechanism that contains rather than eradicates the [[vendetta]], Gans argues that if such systems achieved perfect efficiency, they would eliminate the conditions requiring their own existence. The text extends this logic to data and disciplinary ordering, suggesting that universal data generation and algorithmic transparency would dissolve the distinction between data and non-data, raising fundamental questions about the conditions of meaningful mediation.

## Key Concepts

- **[[Juridical]]** – The ordering system that places ceilings on vendetta through higher-order arbitration while preserving ritual property rights
- **[[Vendetta]]** – The recursive violence that imperial order must contain without necessarily eradicating
- **[[Ritual]]** – The commemoration of origin that, if perfected, would hallow all of life and abolish itself
- **[[Disciplinary]]** – The system of data generation, gathering, and analysis that tends toward totalization
- **[[Originary nomos]]** – The ritually enacted foundation establishing property rights and deferral
- **[[Imperial tensegrity]]** – The structural balance maintaining order through differentiated mediating systems
- **[[Data exchange]]** – The proposed central notion relying on juridical and disciplinary orderings

## Notable Passages

> "The juridical doesn't have to eradicate the vendetta, even though that invariably seems to happen; it just needs to contain it, even while preserving 'cultural' traces."

> "The perfection of the juridical would then be its abolition. Could the same be true, then, of the ritual and disciplinary?"

> "Everything is disciplinary, so, then nothing is—and everything is data, so there's no more data?"

## Questions Raised

1. Does the self-abolishing tendency of perfected systems reveal something essential about how human communities must maintain [[deferral]], or is this specific to historically contingent institutions?

2. If complete [[data]] transparency and algorithmic knowledge create a condition where "everything is data," what distinguishes meaningful [[mediation]] from total saturation?

3. Can we intentionally design systems that achieve their ordering function *without* tending toward their own obsolescence—or is this impossible within GA's logic of the [[center]]?
