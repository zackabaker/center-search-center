## Summary

This post traces the resurgence of "androspheric" thought—an explicitly patriarchal online discipline concerned with restoring masculine authority—back to the originary structure of the [[Big Man]] in primitive societies. The author argues that the tension between the irrepressibility of hierarchical male dominance and modern declarations of equality generates contemporary crises. Drawing on [[Eric Gans]]'s originary theorization, the post suggests that antisemitism and other forms of resentment ultimately stem from a civilizational crisis in [[firstness]]—a repudiation of decisiveness and authority that the androsphere seeks to restore.

## Key Concepts

- **[[Big Man]]**: The originary figure whose accumulation of wealth and followers generates social inequality; the structural template for modern alpha hierarchies.
- **[[Androsphere]]**: The online community of autodidactic thinkers devoted to restoring patriarchal sexual relations through masculine self-discipline.
- **[[Alpha/Omega]]**: Socio-sexual hierarchy endemic to human societies, from Papua New Guinea tribes to modern civilization.
- **[[Firstness]]**: The capacity for decisive authority; its repudiation generates resentment and antisemitic backlash.
- **[[Victimocracy]]**: The civilizational structure that suppresses memory of hierarchical infrastructures while facilitating their resurgence.
- **[[Originary anthropology]]**: The framework using the originary scene to trace social forms from egalitarian hunter-gatherers to market economy.
