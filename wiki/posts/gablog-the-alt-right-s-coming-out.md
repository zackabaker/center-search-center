## Summary

This post analyzes the Alt-Right as a coherent political force emerging around 2015, arguing it functions as the mirror image of SJW progressivism rather than a platform-based movement. The author suggests the Alt-Right's real coherence lies in its "joyful acceptance" of group differences that victimary progressives deny—a refusal of defensive posturing in favor of offensive persistence. Rather than dismissing it as mere reaction, the piece asks what strategic capacity this movement might develop if it attracts elite patronage seeking Western restoration.

## Key Concepts

- [[Victimary progressivism]]: The exploitation of group inequalities by presenting them as evidence of systemic oppression
- [[Mirror image]]: The Alt-Right as symmetrical inversion of SJW logic, accepting rather than denying difference claims
- [[Declarative culture]]: The power to define what can be spoken and asserted in public discourse
- [[Middle rebellion]]: A De Jouvenelian framework where intermediate forces seek patronage from higher authority
- [[Resentment]]: The underlying structure of victimary claims and their reactive counterparts
- [[Rule of law]]: An arbitrary marker in Alt-Right definitions of Western Civilization that obscures rather than clarifies

## Notable Passages

"The Alt-Right is simply the sustained, unremitting, unabashed, thoroughly joyful acceptance of that dare [to assert group differences]."

"The Alt Right is not a defensive attitude and rejects the concept of noble and principled defeat. It is a forward-thinking philosophy of offense, in every sense of that term."

"You can only attract a High patron by looking effective, which the Republican party and conservative movement haven't for a very long time."

## Questions Raised

1. What distinguishes a coherent political movement from mere reactive mirror-imaging? Can the Alt-Right stabilize beyond negation of progressivism?

2. How does the paradox that ethnic expulsions cannot be carried out "in accord with the rule of law" undermine the conceptual foundations of Western Civilization as defined by those pursuing it?

3. If the Alt-Right's real power lies in its capacity to make certain claims speakable again, does its effectiveness depend on institutional capture, or can it persist as a [[declarative culture]] independent of state machinery?
