## How to post this

1. Open the Talk page. For each article, click the **Talk** tab at the top of the article, or go directly to:
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk:Generative_anthropology
   - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talk:Eric_Gans
2. Be logged in to your Wikipedia account (the same account whose user page carries your COI disclosure — adding a short COI note to your user page first is good practice).
3. Click the **"Add topic"** / **"New section"** tab. This opens a fresh section with a Subject/headline field and a body field.
4. The first line of each block (`== ... ==`) is the section heading. If you use the dedicated **Subject/headline** field, paste only the text *between* the `==` marks there and the rest into the body. If you are editing the raw wikitext of the whole page instead, paste the entire block including the `== ... ==` line.
5. Leave the `~~~~` at the very end. Wikipedia converts those four tildes into your signature and timestamp on save — do not replace them manually.
6. Click **Publish changes** (use an edit summary like "Talk-page external-link request with COI disclosure" if prompted).
7. Then wait. Do not edit the article itself. An uninvolved editor will work the COI edit-request queue and accept or decline.

## Note on the Adam Katz / Dennis Bouvard biography question

A standalone Wikipedia biography for Adam Katz / Dennis Bouvard is premature, and given the COI it should not be attempted directly. The bar is [[WP:N]] (general notability) and specifically [[WP:AUTHOR]]: an article needs *significant coverage in multiple reliable, independent secondary sources* — that is, substantial writing *about* Katz/Bouvard by other people (academic reviews, scholarly profiles, mainstream press), not his own books, his Substack, or center.study, all of which are primary or self-published and do not count toward notability. A thin stub built from primary sources would draw COI and deletion scrutiny, and a deletion would make a better-sourced article harder to land later.

The lower-risk path is **accurate naming within the existing articles** rather than a new biography. The current *Generative anthropology* article does not mention Adam Katz or Dennis Bouvard at all. A neutral, well-sourced sentence noting that Adam Katz (writing as Dennis Bouvard) has developed work extending generative anthropology — cited to independent or at minimum verifiable secondary sources — would be a proportionate, defensible contribution, and it too should go through a Talk-page COI edit request (`{{edit COI}}`) with disclosure rather than a direct edit. If independent secondary coverage accumulates over time, a standalone biography can be revisited then, from a stronger position.
